# POLITICAL SYSTEM: RANKS & REPUTATION

## 1. Rank Hierarchy
Progression is the primary long-term goal for the player.

| Index | Rank Name | Req. Gold | Req. Reputation | Privileges |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Shopkeeper** | - | - | Basic trading. |
| 1 | **Trader** | 20,000 | 20 | Build factories. |
| 2 | **Merchant** | 100,000 | 30 | Establish trading offices. |
| 3 | **Traveling Merchant** | 250,000 | 40 | Large convoys. |
| 4 | **Councilor** | 500,000 | 55 | Vote in city council. |
| 5 | **Patrician** | 1,000,000 | 70 | Eligibility for Lord Mayor. |
| 6 | **Lord Mayor** | - | 85 | Elected. Control city taxes/defense. |
| 7 | **Alderman** | - | 95 | Leader of the Hanseatic League. |

## 2. Reputation Calculation
Reputation is city-specific but contributes to a global average.

### 2.1 Gains
- **Trade**: Supplying a city with goods it lacks.
- **Donations**: Cathedral donations (+1 Rep per 1000 Gold).
- **Missions**: Completing tasks for the Town Hall or Tavern (+15 to +100 Rep).
- **Public Works**: Building schools, wells, or upgrading the Cathedral.

### 2.2 Losses
- **Shortages**: If the player is the primary supplier and fails to deliver.
- **Piracy**: Getting caught attacking merchant ships.
- **Financial Default**: Bankruptcy or unpaid loans.

## 3. Election Logic (Lord Mayor)
Occurs annually in the player's home city (December).
- **Voters**: Influential NPCs (Councillors).
- **Factors**: Player's local reputation vs. competing AI reputation.
- **Bribes**: Can be performed in the **Bath House** or **Tavern** to influence councillor votes.

## 4. Alderman Eligibility
- Must be **Lord Mayor** of a city.
- Must have high reputation (80+) in at least 12 different cities.
- Completion of the "Alderman Quests" (e.g., founded a new city, suppressed a major pirate den).
